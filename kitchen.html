Grunnriss kitchen & room planner

Room

Pick Draw wall, click to place corners, double-click or press Esc to finish a wall run. Everything snaps to 5 cm. Walls that meet at a shared corner join cleanly — no gap.
Walls intersect cleanly at corners — no gap, no seam. The cm label on a wall covers only its clear inside span, not the corner it joins into; select a wall to see that exact span marked with a dimension line.
Freehand clicking places corners on the wall's structural centerline, so it won't hit your tape numbers exactly. Once a wall exists, select it and set its Length — inside (cm) field to match what you actually measured — that's the one that reflects real inside-face distance, and the plan label on each wall shows that same inside number.
Type: Partition / Load-bearing toggles which kind new walls are drawn as. Load-bearing walls default to 25 cm and show darker with a hatch fill in 2D and a concrete tone in 3D; partitions default to 10 cm. Change it anytime per wall in the properties panel.

Openings

With + Door, + Window or + Archway active, click a point on a wall to insert it there. Arches get a true curved header in the 3D view — a straight-top door doesn't.

Rooms

Close a wall run back to its start (or box in an area with separate walls), pick Define room, then click inside — you'll be asked to name it and get its floor area. Do this once per room: kitchen now, living room next.

Materials — 3D view only

Floor colour applies to the herringbone and plank options — tile and concrete keep their own fixed look.
Beadboard boards pick up your chosen wall colour. Note: this tool doesn't track which side of a wall faces into a room, so panelling renders on both faces — orbit around and ignore whichever side is actually the outside.
These are stylised, generated patterns for gauging look and scale — not photoreal renders of an actual product SKU.

Add a shape

Each shape you add becomes one piece. Build a coffee table from a slab (box, marble) plus four legs (cylinders, wood) — they merge visually into one piece automatically since they're all part of the same furniture. Click a piece to select it and drag it to move it on the floor. Two handles appear around it: a ring you can drag to spin it, and a small knob on a stem above it you can drag up/down to change its height. Drag empty space to orbit — and clicking empty space also clears the current selection. WASD walks the camera around, Q/E moves it up and down. Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copies and pastes the selected piece, Ctrl+Z undoes.
